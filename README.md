## What I work on

I've been building software for over a decade, across full stack development and mobile engineering, including several years leading a mobile team. My experience includes mobility platforms and SaaS products, alongside custom software for business operations.

Since 2025, I've worked independently as a developer and IT consultant. My recent projects include web and mobile apps, backend services, cloud infrastructure, and integrations with business systems.

I take projects from understanding the problem through architecture, implementation, deployment, and maintenance. Much of that work involves:

- Building apps that work offline and sync when the connection returns.
- Connecting ERP systems, e-commerce, and operational data through APIs.
- Automating manual tasks and keeping systems in sync.
- Tracking down slow responses, unreliable behavior, and inconsistent data.
